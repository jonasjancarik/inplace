For Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT and other agents

Write with your coding agent without breaking your flow.

Open an HTML document your agent wrote and edit it right on the page, like any other document, without breaking its design. When you pause, your agent looks at what you changed and fixes whatever it affects, without waiting to be asked. Anything bigger, you can leave as a comment.

How it works in detail →

How it’s different

Agents now write documents as carefully designed HTML, with colour-coded tables, charts and print layouts. Word or Google Docs would convert all of that into its own format, and much of the design would get lost on the way.

One

It doesn’t wait to be asked

AI in a document editor usually acts when you ask it to. Here your agent looks at your edits whenever you pause and takes care of what they affect, like the total under the amount you just changed.

Two

Your document stays as it is

You edit the file itself, so there's nothing to import or export. Charts, colours and print layout stay exactly as your agent made them, and what you print or send is that same file.

Three

Comments get done while you keep writing

You don’t send a request and wait for the reply. Your agent picks up each comment as you leave it, and the page reloads with the change while you carry on with the rest.

Four

It stays on your computer

The document never leaves your disk. To open it on your tablet, or work on it with someone on the same Wi-Fi, ask your agent to share it on your network.
