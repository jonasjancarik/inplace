You bold a word and add a row on the page. The agent sees the new row and fixes the total, then writes the paragraph you asked for in a comment.

Why not just open it in Word or Google Docs?

Agents now write documents as carefully designed HTML, with colour-coded tables, charts and print layouts. Word or Google Docs would convert all of that into its own format, and much of the design would get lost on the way.

One

Your document stays as it is

You edit the file itself, so there's nothing to import or export. Charts, colours and print layout stay exactly as your agent made them, and what you print or send is that same file.

Two

Your agent sees what you changed

Change an amount and your agent fixes the total that depends on it. Move a section and it updates the “see below” that now points the wrong way, without you having to ask.

Three

You can both work at once

You and your agent can edit the document at the same time, even the same paragraph. If you both change the same words, your version is set aside so you can compare the two and choose.

What you can do on the page

Your changes are saved as you go. For anything bigger, like a new section, a chart or a different layout, leave a comment for your agent.

Type

Click into any paragraph, heading, list item or table cell and change the text.

Add and remove

Press Enter for a new paragraph or list item, or click the + beside a block or table row to add one below. To remove one, empty it and press Backspace once more.

Select text to make it bold, italic or a link, or to turn a paragraph into a heading. Markdown shortcuts such as **bold** and ## work too.

Move

Drag the handle beside a paragraph, list item, table row or section to change its order.

The document stays on your computer. To open it on your tablet, or work on it with someone on the same Wi-Fi, ask your agent to share it on your network.
